# SeriesBumb

## Register

product

## Users

- Visitors browse and search an encyclopedia of Thai cassette releases, songs, artists, and labels.
- Google members mark tapes and songs they like, record tapes they own, and comment.
- Administrators maintain the catalog, collections, images, comments, and member permissions.

## Product Purpose

Preserve and make Thai cassette information from the 1980s through the 2000s easy to find and share. A useful page gives accurate catalog detail, clear relationships among tapes, songs, artists, and labels, and a share preview that works without client JavaScript.

## Brand Personality

Calm, archival, and welcoming. Use concise Thai copy and the familiar density of a music encyclopedia without ornamental clutter.

## Anti-references

- The visual noise of older encyclopedia sites: marble textures, tiny text, advertising boxes, and several competing link colors.
- A music player, streaming service, or commerce storefront. This is a catalog of physical releases.
- Decorative cards and motion that slow down reading or data entry.

## Design Principles

1. Make the catalog entry and its source relationships easy to scan.
2. Keep browsing and administration predictable with familiar navigation, tables, and forms.
3. Let Thai names, years, and album art carry the character of the site.
4. Keep the public catalog usable without JavaScript; enhance interactions progressively.
5. Protect trust by showing only published catalog data and clear member permissions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use Thai as the interface language, WCAG AA text contrast, keyboard controls, visible focus, touch targets of at least 44px, and reduced-motion alternatives. The approved requirements and detailed visual rules are in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-25-seriesbumb-design.md`.
